# Licensing FAQ

## Can I self-host VeySur for my own company's use?

Yes. The [Elastic License 2.0](./LICENSE) lets you use, copy, modify, and
self-host this software freely. The one thing it forbids is providing the
software **to third parties** as a hosted or managed service.

## Does that cover running it for sister companies or other business units?

Yes. Running your own deployment for your organisation's internal use,
including for affiliated companies or business units under common control,
is not "providing the software to third parties." You're not a third party
to your own group. What the licence forbids is offering VeySur as a hosted
product to unaffiliated customers, in other words standing up a competing
managed service.

## Can I fork it, modify it, and redistribute my fork?

Yes, under the same licence terms, including the same restriction on
offering it as a managed service. See [TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md) for
what you can and can't do with the VeySur name and logo when you do.

## Will this ever become open source (OSI-approved)?

It's on the table once the business has an established user base and a
contributor community, and doesn't depend on being the only host for its
revenue. That's not a commitment with a fixed date. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for why the CLA is written to keep
that option open.
