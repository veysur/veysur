# Code of Conduct

This project is a place for people to report problems, suggest improvements and contribute code.
Everyone taking part is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous to other contributors and users, including when you disagree.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming a mistake was deliberate.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the right place: issues for bugs and requests, pull requests for changes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising or sustained off-topic posts.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these
rules. To report a problem, email [admin@veysur.com](mailto:admin@veysur.com). Reports are handled
confidentially.

Security vulnerabilities are reported separately; see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
