VeySur is a survey platform for project administrators. Administrators can build and publish surveys, manage who takes part, and review the results, all from a single interface.

Prefer to watch? The [Video Tutorials](/video-tutorials/) page has short walkthroughs of the main tasks.

This guide covers the features available to administrators, organised into three sections, plus a section for self-hosted installations:

**Reference** documents each part of the survey editor in detail:

- [Survey structure](/reference/survey-editor/survey-structure/): how a survey is organised into groups, questions, and content
- [Question Types](/reference/survey-editor/question-types/simple/): the full range of question formats, from simple text inputs to matrix grids and multi-part questions
- [Preview](/reference/survey-editor/preview/): render the survey as a participant would see it
- [Participants](/reference/survey-editor/participants/): add, import, and contact the people taking your survey
- [Publications](/reference/survey-editor/publications/): publish a survey and manage its lifecycle
- [Responses](/reference/survey-editor/responses/): view, filter, and export collected data
- [Settings](/reference/survey-editor/settings/): configure language, presentation, access rules, and more
- [Statistics](/reference/survey-editor/statistics/): visualise response data with per-question charts
- [Conditional Questions](/reference/survey-editor/conditional-questions/): show or hide questions based on earlier answers
- [Import / Export](/reference/import-export/): move survey data in and out of VeySur
