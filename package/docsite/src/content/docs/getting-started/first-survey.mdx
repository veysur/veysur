This page walks through the full process of setting up a survey from scratch: building the content, previewing it, publishing it, and inviting participants.

## 1. Create a survey

After logging in, you will land on the Surveys list. Click **New Survey** to open the creation form, enter a name for the survey, and confirm. The survey editor opens automatically.

## 2. Name the question group

Questions in VeySur are organised into groups. A group acts as a page or section. There is one group by default. Give the group a name by clicking it in the editor and typing directly.

## 3. Add a question

Within a group, click the **+** button below the group and select **Add Question**. A question type selector appears with question-type categories. Choose a category, then pick a question type. For a first survey, **Text** (under Simple) or **Checkbox** (under Choice) are good starting points.

Once the question is added, click it to focus it. The focused question is highlighted with a background colour. Enter the question text in the editor area. Use the attributes panel on the right to mark the question as **required** or to adjust type-specific options such as minimum/maximum values or answer options.

To add more questions, repeat this step or add a new group.

## 4. Preview the survey

Click **Preview** in the left navigation. The survey is rendered exactly as a participant would see it. Check that the questions read clearly, the order is correct, and any conditional logic behaves as expected. No responses are recorded in Preview.

## 5. Publish the survey

When the content is ready, click **Publish** in the top right of the Edit tab. A modal opens where you can review final settings, optionally add a label (for example, "Wave 1") and notes, then confirm to publish.

If you want to set a start or end date, configure the schedule in the publish modal (you can also configure this on the **Settings** tab under **Publish**). Once a publication is active, its status shows as **Published**.

## 6. Invite participants

Go to the **Participants** tab. Add a participant manually by clicking **Add Participant** and filling in their first name, last name, and email address. To add many participants at once, use the **Import** option to upload a `.csv` file.

Once participants are added, click **Send Invites**. VeySur sends an invitation email to each participant who has not yet received one. A progress indicator shows how many emails have been sent.

Participants receive a link that takes them directly to the survey.

## 7. View responses

Once participants submit the survey, go to the **Responses** tab. A table lists each submission with the participant name, email, completion status, and submission date.

- Use the **search bar** to find a response by ID, name, or email
- Use the **filter toolbar** to narrow by date range, completion status, or publication
- Click a row to open the full response and read each answer in detail
- Click the **download** icon to export all responses as a CSV file

## 8. View statistics

Click **Stats** in the left navigation (bar-chart icon). Each question is displayed as a card showing a chart of answer distribution (bar, horizontal bar, or pie, selectable per chart via its settings icon) and the count and percentage for each answer option.

Use the same date-range, completion status, and publication filters as the Responses tab to focus on a specific subset of submissions.
