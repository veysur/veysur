title: Publish Your Survey
description: How to publish a survey in VeySur so it is ready to collect responses.
---

Publishing a survey creates a publication, which is a snapshot of the survey at a specific point in time. Participants interact with the publication, not the live survey editor. This means you can continue editing the survey without affecting responses already being collected. If the survey content has not changed since the last snapshot, publishing reuses that snapshot rather than creating a new one, so a single snapshot can back more than one publication.

## Before publishing

Confirm the survey content has been reviewed in the [Preview](/reference/survey-editor/preview/) tab, and that any settings such as language, access rules, or presentation options have been configured. See [Settings](/reference/survey-editor/settings/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Invalid conditions block publishing">
  Publishing checks the survey for invalid conditions. See [Conditional Questions](/reference/survey-editor/conditional-questions/) for what makes a condition invalid and how to fix one. If any invalid conditions remain, publishing is blocked and the errors are shown, so they can be corrected before trying again.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the publish modal.** Open your survey and go to the **Edit** tab. Click the **Publish** button in the top-right of the editor. If the survey has been published before, the button shows as **Published** - click it to open the same modal.

2. **Create a publication.** The publish modal has two optional fields. **Label** is a short name to identify this publication, for example "Initial publication" or "Wave 2", useful when creating multiple publications over time. **Notes** is for free-text notes such as the reason for this publication or what changed since the last one.

   Click **Publish** to confirm. The publication is created with a status of **Published** and the current timestamp is recorded as the Published date.

3. **Set a publication window (optional).** If you want the survey to stop accepting responses after a certain date, you can set a start or end date in the publish modal. Alternatively, go to **Settings** and open the **Schedule** section to set these dates before publishing. Responses submitted outside the window are not accepted.

   If no dates are set, the publication remains active until you stop it manually.

4. **Share with participants.** Go to the **Participants** tab to add participants and send invitation emails. See [Invite Participants](/guides/invite-participants/) for full instructions.

</Steps>

## Embedding a published survey

A published open survey can also appear inside a page on another website. The embed setting and the allowed websites are fixed when the survey is published, so publish again after changing either. See [Embed a Survey in a Website](/guides/embed-a-survey/).

## Stopping a publication

When you no longer want to collect responses, go to the **Edit** tab and click the **Published** button in the top-right. A modal opens showing the current publish status. Click **Unpublish** to stop the publication. The status changes to **Stopped** and the Stopped date is recorded. Existing responses are not affected.

<Aside type="caution">
  A stopped publication cannot be restarted. If you need to collect more responses, create a new publication. See [Merge Publication Responses](/guides/merge-publication-responses/) if you want to consolidate responses from multiple publications.
</Aside>

## Publishing after making changes to a survey

If you update the survey after it has been published, the existing publication continues to use the original snapshot. To collect responses against the updated version, create a new publication. The new publication captures the current state of the survey.

## Language content in snapshots

When a publication is created, the translated text for every configured language is frozen into the snapshot separately from the survey structure. Participants receive the text for their assigned language (plus the default language as a fallback) from these frozen records. Changes to language content after publishing do not affect existing publications.
