---

This guide covers adding, reordering, and deleting groups, questions, and content in the survey editor. See [Survey structure](/reference/survey-editor/survey-structure/) for what each attribute means.

## Adding items

To add a group, click the **+** button between existing groups in the structure panel or editor area, then select **Add Group**.

To add a question, click the **+** button at the bottom of a group and select **Add Question**. A type selector opens: choose a category (Simple, Choice, Date and Time, Matrix, or Multi-Part), then a specific type. The question is added at the bottom of the group.

The same **+** button offers **Text content** and **Video (YouTube)** for adding content.

## Editing

Click any item in the editor to focus it. Edit it inline by clicking its title or description text, or through the attributes panel on the right. See [Survey structure](/reference/survey-editor/survey-structure/) for what each attribute means.

## Reordering

Drag items in the structure panel to reorder them. Groups can move relative to one another. A question or content item can move within its group or into a different group. Groups, questions, content, and question sub-parts can also be moved with the up and down arrow buttons shown to the right of a focused or hovered item.

## Deleting

To delete a group, question, or content item, click the delete button shown to its right when it is focused. Deleting a group removes everything inside it.
